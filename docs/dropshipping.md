# Dropshipping Setup

This site uses a dropshipping model with U.S. suppliers. Products are fulfilled and shipped directly from partner warehouses in the United States, so no inventory is kept locally. To implement dropshipping:

1. Partner with reliable suppliers that offer products with Amazonian ingredients.
2. Connect your store via an integration platform (such as Shopify or WooCommerce) to automate order forwarding to suppliers.
3. Ensure each product page clearly communicates shipping times and origin within the U.S.
4. Monitor supplier stock levels to avoid out-of-stock issues.

By leveraging dropshipping, the storefront can focus on marketing and customer service while suppliers handle logistics.
